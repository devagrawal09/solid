`openView`: a list an optimistic store guessed over across an `await` rewinds at every step without throwing. While a view is open, a store node the view did not pin reads its list's pinned frame, a node born under the pin (or at the landing that lifts it) starts from the truth beneath, and a pinned guess carrier is read again though its action ended. Observe and dev tiers only; prod is unchanged.
